Data sources, definitions and validation

Source: nflverse player statistics and NFL Next Gen Stats via nflverse. CC BY 4.0 per nflverse data repository. Filtered, joined and derived ratios by Sideline IQ. Team logos are loaded from ESPN for visual identification only; team abbreviations are shown if images fail. Logos belong to their respective owners. No player headshots included.

Season view uses the original regular-season aggregate and NGS week-0 source records, not averages of weekly percentages. EPA per carry and per target are explicitly defined ratios. NGS percentage fields use source units; traditional share fields use source fractions. PFR advanced data, snaps and routes are not included.
